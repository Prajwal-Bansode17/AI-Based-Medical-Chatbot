# Datasheet for MEDIQ

Following the framework proposed by Gebru et al. (2021), "Datasheets for Datasets."

## Motivation

**Purpose**: MEDIQ was created to provide researchers with a real-world, bilingual (Hindi-English) medical question-answering dataset. It captures authentic user interactions with an AI chatbot deployed at a public Indian medical university.

**Creators**: Dr. Ayush Yadav.

**Funding**: Self-funded.

## Composition

**Instances**: Each instance is a question-answer pair from a chatbot interaction.

**Fields per instance**: 7 core fields (`id`, `question`, `answer`, `timestamp`, `consent`, `consentTimestamp`, `language_detected`) plus 10 AI-enriched fields (`ai_language`, `ai_language_confidence`, `ai_intent`, `ai_medical_relevance`, `ai_sentiment`, `ai_question_translated_en`, `ai_answer_quality`, `ai_topic_tags`, `ai_requires_followup`, `ai_user_type_guess`). AI fields are generated by LLM analysis (Groq Compound via Groq) and provide corrected language detection (including Romanized Hindi identification), English translations, intent classification, and research-oriented metadata.

**Total instances**: See `stats/summary.json` for current count (dataset grows daily).

**Languages**: English (`en`), Hindi (`hi`), and code-mixed Hindi-English (`hi-en`).

**Sensitive data**: No. The dataset contains zero personally identifiable information. The chatbot does not require login or any personal details. No names, emails, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device identifiers are present. The chatbot actively warns users not to share personal or health information.

**Data about people**: No. The data consists of informational queries about university services (OPD timings, admissions, faculty, exams, appointments) and their automated responses.

## Collection Process

**How was data collected**: Data was collected from a publicly deployed AI chatbot on an Indian medical university website. Users voluntarily interact with the chatbot to ask questions about university services.

**Who collected the data**: The chatbot system automatically logs interactions in Firebase Firestore.

**Time period**: April 2025 to present (ongoing).

**Consent**: The chatbot presents a mandatory consent/privacy clause before users can interact. A consent checkbox must be accepted before the chat input is enabled. Consent timestamps are recorded.

**Ethical review**: The data is fully anonymous and contains no clinical, health, or biomedical research data. Under India's DPDP Act 2023, anonymous data is exempt from the Act's scope. ICMR ethical guidelines do not apply as this is administrative/informational data, not clinical research.

## Preprocessing

**Filtering**: Generic messages (greetings, single-word acknowledgments like "hi", "ok", "thanks", "bye") are filtered out. Only substantive questions with real informational content are retained. The filtering preserves questions that contain greetings alongside real queries (e.g., "Hello, what are OPD timings?").

**Language detection**: A simple heuristic is used — presence of Devanagari script characters indicates Hindi, ASCII-only indicates English, and mixed scripts indicate code-mixed (hi-en).

## Uses

**Intended uses**: Research in multilingual NLP, medical QA systems, chatbot evaluation, code-mixing analysis, information retrieval, and user behavior analysis.

**Not intended for**: Training clinical decision-making systems, medical diagnosis, or any system that provides health advice.
